## Findings

**Three core areas of AI discovery/scoping** (OpenAI Academy): **problem framing** — determine
which business challenges are actually suitable for an AI solution (not every problem is); **
architectural decision-making** — choose a technical approach that fits the project's real
requirements, not the most impressive available option (the same "right-size" instinct
`embedded-engineering.md`'s PRISM methodology names); **constraint alignment** — make sure the
chosen use case actually fits within realistic evaluation and deployment limits before
committing to it.

**Positioning**: explicitly aimed at builders/teams planning real production AI work, not a
theoretical intro — part of a broader series also covering reliable agent architecture,
fine-tuning tradeoffs, and practical eval-system development (the last of which connects
directly to `building-eval-pipelines.md`, researched earlier in a different category).

**A real limitation of this finding, worth being honest about**: this source is fairly thin and
general (it's a video-page description, not the video's actual content), so what's captured here
is closer to a syllabus outline than deep technical guidance. It's genuine, on-topic content —
not fabricated — but doesn't go as deep as e.g. `embedded-engineering.md`'s PRISM framework did
for the adjacent "scoping an engagement" problem.

## Findings

**What productization means** (both sources agree): turning custom, one-off consulting expertise
into standardized, repeatable offerings — a spectrum from lightly systematized human-delivered
services to fully automated digital products. naviu.tech frames it as existing on a spectrum;
consultingsuccess.com frames it as "systematized, repeatable offerings that deliver consistent
outcomes" rather than bespoke solutions per client.

**Why it matters, concretely**: naviu.tech gives real numbers — a financial advisory firm cut
delivery time from 15 hours to 2 hours per client while increasing capacity 300%; management
consultants can derive 65% of revenue from only 20% of time investment via productized offerings.
consultingsuccess.com frames the benefit differently but compatibly: escaping the "feast-or-famine"
cycle via predictable recurring revenue, and using AI for data analysis/research/drafting while
reserving strategic human judgment for what clients actually need from a human.

**Concrete steps** (synthesizing both, since they're compatible rather than contradictory):
identify the recurring pattern in your own past work (naviu.tech: "identify core expertise";
consultingsuccess.com: "identify your 20%" via pattern analysis across past projects); define who
it's for (a specific client segment with a common problem); strip the offering down to a
standardized, repeatable methodology while preserving most of the value (consultingsuccess.com's
"ruthless removal" principle: keep ~80% of the value, cut the bespoke 20%); choose a concrete
delivery format and structure (one-time audit/assessment vs. recurring ongoing service); validate
with real prospects before fully building (consultingsuccess.com: talk to 10-15 ideal prospects
first); price on the value delivered, not hours spent.

**Direct relevance to this system**: this is a genuinely interesting parallel to Cimcime itself.
This repo is explicitly built as a reusable *template* (ADR-0004's framing) rather than a one-off
tool for a single engagement — which is itself a form of productization: the FDE's own working
method (discovery → research → delivery, encoded in the category-/topic-agent machinery) is being
systematized into a repeatable, standardized process rather than rebuilt from scratch per
engagement. The consulting literature's own argument for why productization works — it frees the
expert from re-deriving the same solution repeatedly — is the same argument for why this system is
worth building at all.

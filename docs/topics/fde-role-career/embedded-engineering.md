## Findings

**What embedded engineering means, concretely** (Optywise): senior AI/ML engineers embed
directly inside the client's team — sitting in the client's own meetings, coding in the client's
own repositories, carrying responsibility through go-live, not just handing over a
recommendation. Optywise's own framing: they "own the outcome until it runs in production,"
contrasted explicitly against staff augmentation (renting developer time) and traditional
consulting (strategy documents, no production accountability).

**A concrete delivery framework** (Optywise's "PRISM" methodology, ~6 weeks): **P**robe (map the
client's existing systems), **R**ight-size (choose appropriate models/scope for the actual
problem, not the most impressive one), **I**ntegrate (build the real workflow), **S**ecure (add
governance), **M**obilise (deploy and hand off operational control back to the client). This is
a concrete, named instance of the general FDE lifecycle `introduction.md` described abstractly.

**Why this role specifically exploded in 2025-2026** (futureproofing.dev): enterprise AI pilots
routinely fail to reach production — the source cites "95% of enterprise GenAI pilots fail to
produce measurable business value," the same figure (from a different framing) already seen
independently in `roi-ai-impact.md` and `business-acumen.md`'s IBM-sourced statistics. Three
independent sources now converge on roughly this number, which makes it a load-bearing fact for
this whole category, not a one-off claim.

**Three real-world variations of the role** (futureproofing.dev) — directly useful groundwork for
the upcoming `fde-tiers` topic: **Vendor FDEs** (bundled into large platform contracts, report to
the vendor's own product roadmap, not purely the client's); **Solutions Engineers** (support
pre-sales, success measured by closed deals, not production outcomes); **Embedded Engineers**
(dedicated to the client's own roadmap at a flat rate, no vendor agenda). Market cost cited for
direct senior hires: $22K-$38K/month fully loaded.

**When the role actually matters**: specifically "when models meet messy data, undocumented
workflows, and production systems" — i.e. exactly the gap between a working demo and a working
deployment, which is the thread running through every FDE source so far in this category.

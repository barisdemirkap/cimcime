## Findings

**Eight core responsibilities** (fde.academy): requirements gathering (direct customer
interaction, not a spec handed down); solution design (customer-specific, not generic); customization
and integration of existing platforms; production deployment in live environments; testing and
validation for performance/reliability/scale; troubleshooting deployment and integration issues;
cross-team collaboration with internal product/engineering; documentation of solutions and
deployment patterns for future reuse.

**Same ground, reframed as four phases** (uplers.com) — **Embed & Diagnose** (join the customer
team, find the real obstacle beneath the surface-level request — this is the same point
`embedded-engineering.md`'s PRISM "Probe" step made independently); **Build & Ship** (production
code, integrations, API work, infra fixes — not prototypes); **Solve Real Problems** (security
reviews, legacy-system incompatibility, data fragmentation, compliance — concrete, recurring
blockers named explicitly); **Generate Feedback** (patterns across implementations feeding back
into product, same point `the-delta.md`'s "gravel road to paved highway" made).

**The defining tension, named directly** (fde.academy): "high depth of engineering" combined with
"high customer exposure" — most engineering roles are high on one axis and low on the other; the
FDE role is unusual for requiring both simultaneously.

**When an organization should and shouldn't hire for this role** (uplers.com): worth hiring when
enterprise deployments hit real friction that's slowing revenue; **not** worth it for self-serve
products or SMB-focused businesses needing minimal customization — a useful boundary condition
that most of the other sources in this category don't state explicitly.

**Skills mix** (uplers.com): technical depth (Python, APIs, cloud infra) + customer communication
+ domain expertise in regulated industries, plus an ownership mentality and the ability to work
without a complete spec — consistent with every other source in this category so far.
